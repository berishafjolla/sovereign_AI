## 5. Your GDPR Rights

You have the following rights under GDPR:

- **Art. 15** — Right of access: request a copy of your personal data
- **Art. 16** — Right to rectification: correct inaccurate data
- **Art. 17** — Right to erasure: delete your account and data
- **Art. 18** — Right to restriction of processing
- **Art. 20** — Right to data portability: export your data in JSON format
- **Art. 21** — Right to object to processing based on legitimate interests

To exercise any right, use the GDPR tab in your account dashboard or email: **dpo@sovereignai.io**

We will respond within **30 days** (GDPR Article 12).
